patch. Commits and edits in the worktree re-derive that patch through the vendored watch controller, debounced. A
task with no branch shows a `tk work` hint and nothing else.

vendored watch controller).

| task metadata (branch, base) → git argv | The branch name and pinned base come from the task record and reach `gitBranchHead` / `gitDiffPatch` |

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-04-01 | Denial of Service | live.ts watch plan | medium | mitigate | `ignoredRoots` drops `node_modules` and `.git`. Events go through the controller's quiet (250 ms) and maximum (1000 ms) debounce. Checks are serialized, so a refresh never overlaps another. The watcher closes on unmount and when the session is retargeted. |
