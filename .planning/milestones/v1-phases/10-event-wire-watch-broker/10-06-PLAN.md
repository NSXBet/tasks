<objective>
Decision IDs: D-01..D-05 as numbered in 10-01-PLAN.md `<objective>`. This plan implements D-04 (the pi/omp extension
tails the log and surfaces comments, approvals and sent review batches to the agent automatically) and the tool-calling
half of D-02 (agents wait through a tool call with their own cursor).

**As an** agent working a task in pi/omp, **I want** review comments my reviewer sends, approvals, rejections and plain
comments on my tasks to arrive in my session on their own, with a brief of what I missed at session start, **so that**
step 11 of the loop ("picks up automatically: comment → back to work; approval → merge worktree, close task") needs no
polling.

Purpose: ROADMAP Phase 10 SC-1 (many agents watch via tool calling) and SC-3 (comments, approvals and sent batches
picked up automatically). WIRE-01, WIRE-03.

Not planned: the spec's "turn-end guard" (consumers, omp/pi). It is not in 10-CONTEXT.md, the ROADMAP success criteria
or WIRE-01..03.

  than re-implementing.

<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| log (reviewer-authored text) → agent prompt | comment bodies are injected into the agent's session as user messages |
| agent tool call → `tasks_wait` | query and timeout come from the model |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-10-28 | Tampering | control/ANSI sequences in injected text | medium | mitigate | `inboxMessage` / `formatEvent` strip C0/C1 and ANSI CSI from user fields; unit-tested |
| T-10-29 | Elevation of privilege | prompt injection via reviewer comment | medium | accept | comments are by design instructions from the workspace's reviewer; messages are framed as review comments with author, and the agent acts only through `tk` (spec: the extension never touches the log itself) |
| T-10-30 | Denial of service | message flood from bursts | low | mitigate | per-task 250ms coalescing; one brief at session start; only followed tasks and verdict/comment types |
| T-10-31 | Denial of service | `tasks_wait` held forever | low | mitigate | default 30 min, max 2 h timeout |
</threat_model>
