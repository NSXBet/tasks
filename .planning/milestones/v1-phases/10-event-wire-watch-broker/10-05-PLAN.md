Decision IDs: D-01..D-05 as numbered in 10-01-PLAN.md `<objective>`. This plan implements D-03 for the terminal: the
TUI tails the one log, read-only, and its board and notifications come from it (spec §10 consumers: "Tails the log
read-only — the board repaints live · Notification when a task reaches ready-to-review or gains a comment").

**As a** reviewer in the terminal UI, **I want** the board to repaint from the event log and a status-bar toast when a
task is ready for review, approved, rejected or commented, **so that** TUI, web and agents share one live stream.

Purpose: ROADMAP Phase 10 SC-2 (TUI half) and SC-3 (comments and approvals visible). WIRE-02, WIRE-03.

  `TuiStore.watch` signature is at ~:407. `boardSignature` (~:112) is used only by that poll (grep before removing).

  (3s status-bar toast) and `setLastEvent`.

  so those tests need no transport option.
